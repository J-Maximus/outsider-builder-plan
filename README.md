A skills map and training plan for people who came to software from outside it and are directing coding agents to ship something other people will use. It is built on Andrew Ng's AI Engineering Skills Map and one outsider's plan against it. It assumes you will not become a programmer. It does not assume you can skip what programmers know. `PLAN.md` is the guide: the map, the rules that don't depend on your hours, a plan by hours, and the sources worth the time. `TEMPLATE.md` is the blank you copy and fill in — where you stand, what you owe, your next ninety days. `EXAMPLE.md` is the template as the author filled it in, dated.

It is public in order to be corrected. The goal is to turn the guide and the template into the best possible documents for someone starting out, or already mid-stream, as an outsider builder, and one builder's experience is not enough to get there.

## How to use this

- Read §0 of `PLAN.md` and answer its three questions before anything else.
- Find the tier that matches your hours.
- Copy `TEMPLATE.md` into your own repository (not this one) and fill it in against the guide, section by section, dating everything.
- When it's filled in, post a link to it in the pinned Discussion — that is what Discussions here is for, and reading other people's is the fastest way to calibrate your own.
- Open an issue here if you find something in the guide wrong, missing, or dangerous.

## How to correct it

Corrections are welcome as issues using the template, and so are pointers to resources that do part of this better. The author reads every one. Changes land as dated entries in `CHANGELOG.md`, never as silent edits, and are credited by handle.
